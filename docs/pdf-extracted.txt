1 Migration Factor overview
According to its website, Migration Factor is a migration and visa consultancy based in Perth, 
Western Australia. The company says it supports students, professionals and families with 
overseas education, employment, permanent residence and family-reunification pathways. The 
website presents opportunities related to Australia as well as the United States, Canada, New 
Zealand, Europe and the United Kingdom.
The website specifically mentions clients from Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka and Nepal. 
The company describes its services as personalized, professional and transparent visa support. 
Its main message is: “Your Future Starts with the Right Destination.”
1.1 Mission and vision
Mission
The website says the company aims to help people achieve their dreams of studying, working 
and living in Australia through expert migration guidance and ethical consulting.
Vision
The company aims to become a trusted name in migration consultancy by helping thousands 
of clients build new beginnings beyond borders.
1.2 Figures highlighted on the website
The website displays 24 years of experience, a 4.8/5.0 customer rating, 1,500+ visa-approved 
customers and a promise to respond within 48 hours. The home page also states a 
97% immigration success rate. These figures are website marketing claims and should be 
independently verified before relying on them.

3 Visa and immigration services
3.1 Education and visitor services
• Student Visa — Subclass 500  — Support for education in Australia and selected European 
destinations, including university selection, GTE/SOP , financial documents and visa preparation.
• Visitor Visa — Subclass 600 — Guidance for visiting Australia, family visits and travel planning.
• Australian Citizenship — Guidance through the citizenship application journey.
3.2 Family and relationship pathways
• Partner Visa  — Onshore 820/801, Offshore 309/100 and Prospective Marriage Visa 300; 
relationship evidence, statements and sponsor documentation.
• Parent Visa — Aged Parent 804, Contributory Parent 173/143/884/864 and Sponsored Parent 
870 pathways.
3.3 Skilled, employment and business pathways
• Employer Sponsored Visa  — Skills in Demand 482, Employer Nomination 186, Training 407, 
Regional 494 and Temporary Work 400 pathways, including employer sponsorship, nomination 
and documentation support.
• GSM & Skilled Visa — Skilled Independent 189, Skilled Nominated 190, Skilled Work Regional 
491 and National Innovation 858; EOI, points strategy and skills-assessment support.
• Business Migration — Business Innovation & Investment 188, permanent 888, Investor 188B, 
Significant Investor 188C and Entrepreneur 188E pathways are mentioned on the website.
3.4 Humanitarian and review services
• Humanitarian & Protection — Protection Visa 866, Refugee, Special Humanitarian Program 
202, Women at Risk 204 and emergency humanitarian cases.
• Appeals & Reviews  — Support after refusal or cancellation, including decision-letter review, 
evidence, written submissions, AAT representation and, in some cases, Ministerial Intervention.
Legal caution
Visa subclasses, points, deadlines, health and character requirements, and appeal rights can 
change. Confirm current information through the Australian Department of Home Affairs 
and an appropriately registered migration professional before taking action.
4 IELTS and PTE coaching
According to the website, IELTS coaching covers Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking, with 
mock tests, personalized strategies and tutor feedback. PTE coaching focuses on computer-
based exam patterns, smart techniques, time management and practice materials. Both 
programs mention online classes, tutor-led sessions and resource material.
Before choosing IELTS or PTE, applicants should check the official requirements of their institution 
and visa pathway. Some coaching pages contain template-like or outdated wording, so test rules 
and result timelines should be verified through official IELTS or Pearson sources.

5 Practical next steps for clients
1. Clarify your objective: study, work, partner , parent, visitor , business or protection.
2. Prepare your basic profile: passport, age, education, work history, English result, family details 
and current visa status.
3. Request an eligibility assessment and ask for the pathway, estimated timeline, professional 
fee and government charges in writing and separately.
4. Obtain a document checklist and confirm validity, translations, certification and evidence 
requirements.
5. Cross-check any success-rate, guaranteed-approval or fast-outcome claim against official rules 
and signed engagement terms.
6. If you have received a refusal or cancellation, check the deadline immediately and contact a 
registered professional without delay.
7. Read and verify all forms, statements, financial evidence and personal details before 
lodgement.
